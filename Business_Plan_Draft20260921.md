# SideBuySide: A Smarter, Simpler Way to Shop Online

## Shop the Whole Market, Not Just One Store.

Online shopping gives consumers access to more products than ever before, but having more choices does not always make choosing easier. When similar products are sold across multiple e-commerce platforms, shoppers often have to move back and forth between websites, compare inconsistent product information, read countless reviews, and keep track of different prices and promotions. The process can quickly become time-consuming and frustrating. Some shoppers spend far too much time researching, while others give up and make a purchase that may not fully meet their needs.

Today’s recommendation systems add another challenge. Most operate as “walled gardens,” using what they know about a shopper only within their own platforms and product catalogs. The better a retailer understands a customer, the more relevant its recommendations may become; but those recommendations are still limited to what that retailer sells. A shopper’s preferences, purchase history, and interests do not easily follow them from one platform to another.

**SideBuySide** is designed to break down these barriers and put shoppers back in control. By bringing products, shopping data, and personalized recommendations together, SideBuySide helps consumers discover the best options across the broader market instead of restricting them to a single retailer’s selection.

At the center of the SideBuySide experience is **SideShelf**, a unified shopping cart where shoppers can collect and organize products from different retailers. Rather than keeping multiple browser tabs open or trying to remember which website offered the best deal, users can view their options in one convenient place.

Within SideShelf, **SideKick** acts as an AI-powered shopping companion. It compares products `side by side` and analyzes the details that matter, including prices, specifications, features, warranties, ratings, reviews, promotions, and alternative options. SideKick turns scattered and often inconsistent information into a clear comparison, making it easier for shoppers to understand the trade-offs between products.

**SideKick Chat** makes the experience even more natural. Instead of searching through product pages and long customer reviews, shoppers can discuss their options in an interactive conversation. They can ask questions, explain what matters most to them, and receive personalized recommendations in real time. Whether a shopper cares most about performance, value, quality, style, safety, or sustainability, SideKick can synthesize information from across the market and translate it into clear, practical guidance.

Shopping decisions are not always made alone, so SideBuySide also makes collaboration easy. Users can share their SideShelves with friends and family, exchange opinions, and invite others to join the conversation. They can also consult specialized **SideKick personas**, such as an Expert, Bestie, Mom, or Financial Advisor. Each persona approaches the decision from a different perspective, helping shoppers consider factors they may otherwise overlook. By turning product research into an engaging conversation, SideKick helps people make complex decisions with greater confidence and less effort.

Once a shopper has chosen what to buy, SideBuySide **simplifies the purchasing process** as well. Users can consolidate purchases from multiple retailers and complete checkout through a single interface. Behind the scenes, SideBuySide organizes the orders and submits them to the appropriate merchants. This eliminates the inconvenience of managing several carts, accounts, and checkout processes.

SideKick continues to provide value throughout the entire shopping journey. Over time, it learns each shopper’s preferences, identifies relevant alternatives, highlights attractive offers, and provides recommendations based on individual needs and goals. Because its guidance extends across retailers, SideKick can focus on what is best for the shopper, not simply what is available from one platform.

In categories such as fashion, beauty, and cosmetics, SideKick can further reduce uncertainty through **virtual try-on capabilities** and personalized feedback. Shoppers can visualize products before purchasing, consider how well different items match their preferences, and receive AI-powered advice to help them make more confident choices.

By breaking down the silos between e-commerce platforms, SideBuySide creates a more connected and shopper-centered experience. Product discovery, comparison, personalized recommendations, collaboration, and checkout all come together in one intelligent ecosystem. The result is a simpler way to shop: less time spent searching, less uncertainty when choosing, and better purchasing decisions across the entire market.

## Growing with the Next Generation of Shoppers

SideBuySide’s go-to-market strategy begins with college freshmen, a highly connected group entering a new stage of independence. For many freshmen, starting college is the first time they are making everyday purchasing decisions on their own. They must choose what to buy for their dorm rooms, classes, personal care, technology, clothing, food, and social lives, often while managing a limited budget and navigating unfamiliar choices.

These students also tend to have similar needs at the same time. As they move into campus housing, thousands of freshmen may be shopping for many of the same products, from bedding and storage solutions to laptops, headphones, school supplies, and dorm essentials. Yet they often make these decisions with limited experience, relying on scattered online research, social media recommendations, advice from friends, and long lists of products from different retailers.

This environment makes college campuses an ideal starting point for SideBuySide. Students live close to one another, communicate frequently, and naturally exchange product recommendations. A SideShelf created by one student can be shared with roommates, classmates, friends, and family members. SideKick Chat can help groups compare options, coordinate shared purchases, and decide which products best fit their needs and budgets. Shopping becomes not only easier, but also more collaborative and social.

The campus community also creates a powerful opportunity for **organic growth**. When one student finds a useful product, creates a SideShelf, or receives a helpful recommendation, that experience can quickly spread through residence halls, student organizations, classes, and social circles. Rather than acquiring users individually across a broad and fragmented market, SideBuySide can build concentrated communities of shoppers within each campus.

**This model is highly repeatable.** Although every college has its own culture, freshmen across campuses experience many of the same transitions and purchasing needs. A successful approach at one university can be adapted and introduced at another using similar product collections, student partnerships, campus activities, and referral programs. Each campus becomes a focused market where SideBuySide can build awareness, encourage collaboration, and develop lasting user relationships.

The opportunity also **renews every academic year**. As one class advances, a new group of freshmen arrives with the same need to furnish their rooms, prepare for classes, manage their budgets, and make independent purchasing decisions. This creates a recurring entry point for SideBuySide to welcome new users while continuing to serve existing ones.

Our goal is not simply to help students shop during their first year. It is to grow with them. As freshmen become sophomores and move into apartments, their needs expand to include furniture, kitchenware, household essentials, transportation, travel, and professional clothing. As they approach graduation, they begin shopping for interviews, relocation, new jobs, and independent living. Later, their needs may evolve toward major life purchases, including homes, vehicles, weddings, and products for their own families.

Throughout this journey, SideKick can continuously learn each user’s preferences, priorities, and purchasing history. The relationship becomes more valuable over time: students receive increasingly personalized, market-wide recommendations, while SideBuySide develops a deeper understanding of how their needs change across different stages of life.

By starting with college freshmen, SideBuySide can serve consumers at the moment they begin forming independent shopping habits. The campus provides a concentrated, social, and repeatable environment for early growth, while each incoming class creates a new opportunity to expand. SideBuySide will begin with students, support them through college, and grow alongside them for years to come.
